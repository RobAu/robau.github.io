Pages

Small browser games and toys. Everything runs in the page, nothing is installed.

Games

  • Tinyhold

    A hamlet that builds itself. Watch a valley settle and grow; follow a villager or click a building.

  • Creatures

    Evolution sandbox: creatures with real genomes eat, breed, hunt and split into species while you shape the land.

  • Trapdoor

    Thirteen fake apps, each hiding a dark pattern. Do the task without getting tricked, then learn the trick.

  • Sleight of Mind

    A mentalist predicts your choices. Pick freely, then see which nudge was used on you and the science behind it.

  • Mothlight Hollow

    Idle game on a floating night island: glims build, moths nibble, stardust and dreams pile up.

  • Snapback

    Stretch a rubber band between two pins to sweep up stars across 26 cork-board levels.

  • Contraptionary

    Build chain-reaction machines from planks, fans, springs and ropes across 12 physics puzzles.

  • Hotel Nocturne

    Comic-gothic text adventure: survive one night in a hotel run by a cat. Nineteen endings.

  • Little Guildhall

    Gather goods, build workshops and court patrons for prestige in a 24-turn medieval guild game.

  • Undergrowth

    Deck-building bug battler: place insects and spells on a tray, survive eight waves of invaders.

  • Reviver

    Hamster platformer: jump, squash bugs, collect seeds, beat bosses and buy upgrades between runs.

  • Ant m up

    Lead a growing ant trail through mazes; gather enough ants to bridge gaps and push rocks.

  • Spellguard

    Draw hiragana to cast elemental spells. Eight lessons teach 16 characters with score and streaks.

  • Echo Stars

    Listen to a tune and tap the stones to echo it. Ear training with stars, songs and unlocks.

  • Fiesta de Palabras

    Spanish word games for kids: pictures, listening, spelling tiles and memory across levelled word packs.

Toys

  • Dream Music

    Touch a liquid canvas to wake voices. Higher sings higher, more fingers add more voices.

  • Smoke, Remembering

    A short poem written in smoke that flows from word to word. Touch to stir it.

  • Grass Roots

    Living meadow cross-section: plant flowers and trees, watch roots, worms and weather through a day.

  • Hum Lane

    Hum into the microphone and steer your pitch line into the green lane to match notes.

  • Tidepool

    Calm water you can ripple with a touch, with an optional breathing guide.
