How to play
  • Each battle you draw 6 cards and get food to spend. Play bugs onto your 3×2 tray and spells onto your bugs, then press Fight.
  • Bugs act in order of speed. Melee bugs only strike when nothing of yours stands in front of them in their column. They hit the nearest enemy, same column first.
  • Ranged bugs strike from anywhere. Flyers strike from anywhere and go for the back row.
  • Win to add a new card to your deck. Lose and your colony takes damage, then you try the wave again with a fresh hand.
  • Survive 8 waves, including the giant hornet, to win the season.
  • Tap a bug on your tray to move it, or to recall it to your hand before the fight.
